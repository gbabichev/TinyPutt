Support

Help with Tiny Putt.

Tiny Putt is a small, offline mini-golf game. Here are the answers to the most common questions.

Contact

Questions, bugs and ideas are handled through GitHub Issues: open an issue.

Please don't include personal details in a report.

Common questions

How do I putt?

Put a finger down anywhere on the screen, drag away from the direction you want to go, and let go. The harder you pull, the harder the putt: the ring around the ball shows the power, and a dotted line shows the route. Pull back only a little, or slide your finger back to the start, and nothing happens.

What happens in water or lava?

You add one stroke and the ball goes back to the spot you last putted from.

How do I play an earlier hole, or skip ahead?

Choose Levels on the main menu. Every hole is shown as a picture, with your best score on it. Tap any hole to play it, whether you've finished it or not. When you finish, choose Next level, Play again, or go back to the list.

Does replaying a hole change my progress?

No. Your place in the campaign only moves when you play the hole you're up to. Replays and holes played out of order only update your best score for that hole.

What if a hole is too hard?

Use the skip button to move on to the next one. A skipped hole shows as "skipped" in the Levels list, so you can come back to it.

How do several players share one device?

Add players from the main menu. Each player has their own place in the campaign and their own best scores. Choose Versus to play the same hole one after another: player 1 plays until the ball is in, then player 2, and so on. Versus doesn't change anyone's campaign progress.

How do I unlock more ball colours?

Four colours are free. The other eight are earned as you play, by finishing holes, getting holes in one, finishing under par, and playing and winning Versus holes.

Are my scores saved?

Yes. Your level, best scores and stats are kept on the device. They aren't sent anywhere, so deleting the app removes them.

Why does sound or haptics not work?

Both have a switch in the settings. Haptics are only available on iPhone.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, and Mac with macOS 15 or later.

What to include in a report

  • Your device and its system version
  • The hole number, if it's about a particular hole
  • What you did just before the problem
  • A screenshot, if it helps
