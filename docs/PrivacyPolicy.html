Privacy Policy

Tiny Putt collects nothing.

Tiny Putt is built to know as little as possible. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no network access.

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version

  • No data collection. The developer receives no information about you.
  • No ads and no tracking. Tiny Putt includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
  • No network. The app works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.
  • No purchases. There are no in-app purchases.

What is stored on the device

To remember your progress, Tiny Putt saves a small amount of data on the device itself: your settings (sound and haptics), the players you add and their names, each player's level, best strokes per hole, ball colour and a few counts (holes finished, holes in one, holes under par, Versus holes played and won).

This data never leaves the device. It is removed when the app is deleted.

Permissions

Tiny Putt does not ask for access to your location, photos, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal information.

Children's privacy

Tiny Putt does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no ads, no links that leave the app, and no way to buy anything.

Sounds

All sounds are generated inside the app. No audio is ever recorded or sent anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the new version will be posted on this page and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through GitHub Issues.
